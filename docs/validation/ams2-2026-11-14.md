## Failures and operational lessons

| Finding | Action taken | Remaining limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fuel exhaustion occurred outside an ended recording | Added a loaded-fuel/return-reserve procedure and independent continuous capture | Earlier missed telemetry cannot be reconstructed; automatic audible fuel alerts are not implemented |
| Coarse Windows Capture Time clock | AMS2 capture origin and tick timing changed to `time.perf_counter()`; strict timestamp invariants retained | Exact cause of the failed buffer is moderate confidence because that buffer was lost; ACC and auxiliary clocks were not changed |
| Shared rotating log conflict | Isolated observer/clock logs operationally | General multi-process logging still needs hardening |
| Windows long paths | Short test temporary directory and a separate extended-path analysis retry | Path budgets and deployment layout need product-level treatment |
| Live feedback helper status-file replacement failed | Preserved exit 1, samples and correction of its misleading default timeout label | Helper stopped; independent archive and main recorder continued. Sharing contention is a suspected cause, not established proof |
| Repeated manual coordination burden | Added a driving/waiting clock and moved toward passive observation | Avoid asking the driver to repeat tests unless a specific unresolved condition requires it |

Runtime inspection confirmed that this Python build's `time.monotonic()` used a 15.625 ms-resolution clock and `time.perf_counter()` used a high-resolution performance counter. The fix did not add artificial timestamp increments, drop repeated ticks or weaken validation. Confidence is high in the clock-resolution finding and in the successful subsequent live captures; it is moderate that coarse-clock duplication explains the exact earlier failure.

## Continuous capture and driver clock

The new [continuous telemetry collector](../continuous-telemetry.md) targets 60 Hz, independently of individual Recorded Sessions and seat-time tracking. It preserves full supported shared-memory pages with a matching before/copied/after even sequence, compressed segments, hashes, freshness and explicit coverage gaps. Its first reviewed live segment contained 3,465 samples at **57.75 Hz**, not a guaranteed 60 Hz. Missing/stale mappings, unsupported layouts and low disk reserve must remain visible as gaps.

The current-user logon task was registered and launched successfully. Actual new-logon, reboot and sleep/resume recovery were **NOT RUN** as part of this visit. Simulator disappearance/recovery was tested with fake sources, not by forcing a fresh live restart. Continuous capture does not imply model-agent persistence or uninterrupted operation while the machine is unavailable.

The [driver clock](../driver-clock.md) records driving, non-driving, pit movement, transition and unknown time. One final bounded segment measured 17m32.328s total: 13m04.799s driving, 3m55.701s non-driving, 24.329s pit movement, 1.624s transition and 5.875s unknown. Earlier time belongs to other segments or was unmeasured; one earlier clock restart left a 4.85-second gap. These totals are not a whole-visit baseline, and non-driving time does not distinguish intentional rest from product-caused waiting. The clock was stopped when the driver ended the visit; continuous telemetry was left enabled under the driver's stated preference.
